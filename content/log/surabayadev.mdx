**Context.** SurabayaDev Developer Battle 2025 — a national data analysis competition focused on hotel booking and cancellation patterns. The problem: a hotel was losing revenue to cancellations, but the drivers hadn't been identified, so nothing could be acted on.

**My role.** Data analyst — from exploring the raw data through to recommendations that could actually drive decisions.

**What I worked on.** EDA on booking and cancellation patterns to find where the loss actually happens, identifying the drivers of revenue loss, then building interactive dashboards and recommendations so the findings wouldn't stop at a notebook.

**Result.** 3rd place nationally. The judges called out the storytelling and analytical depth.

**More detail.** This work has its own case study: [Hotel Cancellation Analysis](/projects/hotel-cancellation-analysis).
