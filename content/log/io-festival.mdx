**Context.** I/O Festival 2026 by BEM FTI Universitas Tarumanagara, a national competition. I competed in the web development category. Result: 1st place nationally.

**My role.** Team leader — I led the team that built **PulangKeMerek**.

**What we built.** A **B2B** platform that helps FMCG brands meet their Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) duties — the obligation for producers to take responsibility for the packaging they put into the world. The line we pitched on stage: turning waste into data, and handing responsibility back to the brand that made it.

**One lesson.** You don't win by shipping the most features — you win because the problem was defined most clearly. That's something I now carry into every project.

**The core idea is deliberately left out.** PulangKeMerek is now being prepared for a patent, so the main mechanism stays off this page.
